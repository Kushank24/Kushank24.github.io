openrewrite / rewrite-spring
PR #688 · Merged

Spring JDBC — queryForLong → queryForObject Migration Recipe

Authored an automated OpenRewrite recipe for the rewrite-spring library that migrates deprecated JdbcTemplate.queryForLong() calls — removed in Spring 5.3 — to the modern queryForObject() equivalent. The recipe performs direct AST manipulation on Java method invocations and was merged into the Spring Framework 6 upgrade composite, shipping to thousands of downstream projects.

Research Papers

ResearchGate · 2021 Quantitative Finance · Machine Learning

Stock Price Prediction using Hidden Markov Models and understanding the nature of underlying Hidden States

Investigated the application of Hidden Markov Models (HMMs) to financial time-series forecasting, modelling stock price sequences as observations emitted by latent market regimes. The paper characterises the nature of the inferred hidden states — mapping them to interpretable market conditions such as bull, bear, and sideways trends — and evaluates prediction accuracy against baseline methods. Grounded in measure-theoretic probability and stochastic processes from an M.Sc. Mathematics thesis at BITS Pilani.
